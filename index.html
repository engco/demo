What is AP Lang?

AP English Language and Composition is a college-level course focused on how writers use language to inform, persuade, and connect with an audience. You'll read essays, speeches, and arguments from a wide range of writers, and you'll practice building your own clear, evidence-based arguments.

This isn't a literature class — we're not analyzing novels for symbolism. We're studying rhetoric: the choices writers and speakers make to be effective, and how to make those same choices well in your own writing.

Skills you'll build

  • Rhetorical analysis

    Breaking down how a piece of writing or speech achieves its purpose — tone, evidence, structure, and appeals to the audience.

  • Argumentative writing

    Building your own position on an issue and defending it with logic and evidence, not just opinion.

  • Synthesis writing

    Reading multiple sources on a topic and weaving them into a single, coherent argument of your own.

  • Timed writing

    Planning and drafting a strong essay under a deadline — a skill that pays off well beyond the AP exam.
