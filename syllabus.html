Syllabus & Expectations

Course expectations

  • Reading load: Expect regular non-fiction reading — essays, articles, and speeches — most weeks.
  • Writing frequency: You'll write often, in both short in-class pieces and longer take-home essays.
  • Revision matters: First drafts are a starting point. Feedback and rewriting are part of the process, not a punishment.
  • Participation: Class discussion is where a lot of the real learning happens — come ready to talk about what you read.
